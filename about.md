# Auto Decorator

Decorate your level layout with one click.

Open the level editor and press **Auto Deco** next to Build / Edit / Delete.

- **Change style** - flip through the styles with a live preview on the section you are looking at.
- **Colours** - edit Background, Blocks, Outline, Accent and Glow for any style (hex or colour picker), keep your own custom palette per style.
- **Structure layout** - adds grey pillars and walls you can edit before decorating.
- **Decorate level** - fills the empty space around your layout with matching decoration. Your own objects are never moved, changed or covered.
- **Tools** - undo the last decoration in one step, tempo sync, verify presets.

Works offline, collects no data, and decorations are plain editor objects you can keep editing.

Add your own styles without recompiling: see the README ("Preset format").
